Oche Up! support

Frequently asked questions and contact

Oche Up! keeps score for your darts games. Here are the answers to the most common questions; for anything else, write to us directly.

Getting started

How do I start a game?

On the home screen, tap "+", pick the game and its options, enter the players (the order of players is the playing order; you can change it or shuffle it), then tap "Start". In Killer, a screen then asks you to assign a sector to each player.

How do I enter darts?

Tap the pad: "X2" or "X3" (or the Single, Double, Triple buttons depending on the layout) then the number, or "Bull" and "B-eye" for the bull, "Miss" for a missed dart. Tap ✓ to validate the visit, ← to delete the last dart. In settings you choose between the classic pad and the X2 / X3 pad.

Which games are supported?

301, 501, 701 and 1001 (with optional double in and double out), Cricket (Score, No score, Cut throat), Random Cricket, Around the Clock, Shanghai and Killer. The rules of each game are in the app, from the new game screen.

Voice entry

How do I call my darts?

Tap the microphone button, to the right of ✓, then call your darts: "treble twenty, nineteen, double twelve", "bull", "bullseye", "miss"… Each recognised dart appears in the visit as you speak; you can correct it before validating with ✓. The first time, iOS asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition.

Recognition got it wrong: what can I do?

Speak clearly, number by number, with a short pause between darts. A misheard number is fixed with ← or by tapping the pad. The language listened to is the app's language (French or English): change it in settings if needed.

Does voice entry need a connection?

When your iPhone or iPad supports it for the chosen language, recognition happens on the device, with no connection. Otherwise iOS may use Apple's speech recognition service. See the privacy policy.

Games

How do the games work?

301 to 1001: count down to zero; if a visit goes past what's left, it is cancelled (bust). Cricket: close 20 to 15 and the bull by hitting each sector three times, and score points while an opponent hasn't closed it. Around the Clock: hit the sectors in order, 1 to 20 then the bull. Shanghai: each round you aim at the round's number; single, double and treble of the same number in one visit make a Shanghai. Killer: each player has a sector; hitting your own double makes you a Killer, then you take lives from opponents by hitting their doubles. The last player alive wins.

How do I fix a mistake?

Tap the undo arrow at the top of the game to cancel the last visit. The game history lets you review every visit.

How do I replay a game?

In a game's "···" menu, choose "Rematch": the new game uses the same players and settings.

How do I delete a game?

Swipe the game to the left in the list, or open it then "···" → "Delete". With the selection icon you can delete several at once.

Statistics, tournaments and PDF

How do I create a tournament?

On the home screen, tap the trophy icon then "+", name the tournament and pick the players. You can then start games inside the tournament, or assign existing games: tap the selection icon, pick the games, then "+" with the tournament icon.

How do I share results as a PDF?

In a game or a tournament, use the share button. In the list, the selection icon lets you tick several games and export a PDF with ranking, history and statistics. The statistics page has its own export.

Your data

Do I need an account?

No. Oche Up! has no account and no server. Your games are saved on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, synced between your devices through your own iCloud.

My games don't sync

Check that you are signed in to the same Apple account on your devices, that iCloud is enabled for the app (Settings → your name → iCloud → Oche Up!) and that your iCloud storage isn't full. Syncing can take a few moments.

How do I change the app language?

In the app's settings: "Auto" follows your device language, or pick French or English.
