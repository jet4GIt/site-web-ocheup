Privacy policy

Oche Up! — last updated: November 23, 2026

Oche Up! is an app that keeps score for darts games. This page explains what the app stores, where it lives, and what is never transmitted. In short: the publisher collects no data.

No data collected

Oche Up! has no user account and no server. The publisher does not receive, collect or store any data about you: not your games, not your players' names, not your voice, not your location, not an advertising identifier. The app works offline; the only communications with the Internet are iCloud sync and, possibly, speech recognition, described below: they are carried out by Apple on your behalf and you control them.

What the app stores on your device

Your games, player names, tournaments, scores, entered darts and settings are stored in the app's space, on your device.

Like other app data, they may be included in your iPhone or iPad backups (iCloud or computer) if you have enabled them. They are then handled by Apple, according to your settings, not by the publisher.

iCloud sync

If your device is signed in to iCloud, Oche Up! syncs your games, players and tournaments, as well as your pad setting, so you can find them on your other devices (iPhone, iPad) signed in to the same Apple account. This data is kept in the private database of your iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit technology: only you can read it. The publisher has no access to it and receives no copy. This sync is handled by Apple, under its own terms and your iCloud storage.

Without an iCloud account, or if you turn iCloud off for the app in Settings, everything stays on the device.

Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only when you tap the microphone button to call your darts, and only while listening. The app keeps no recording: the sound is only used to get the text of your call, which is turned into darts on your device. Recognition is provided by Apple (iOS Speech framework). When your device supports it for the chosen language, it runs entirely on the device, with no data sent. Otherwise, iOS may send the audio to Apple's servers to transcribe it, under Apple's privacy policy; the publisher never has access to it. If you deny microphone or speech recognition permission, the rest of the app works normally.

Results and sharing

The share function builds a PDF on your device (ranking, history, statistics), then opens the iOS share sheet. You choose the recipient and tap "Send": nothing is transmitted before that.

No ads, no tracking

Oche Up! is free. It shows no ads, uses no tracking or analytics tools, and includes no third-party software. In the App Store listing, the "App Privacy" section therefore says "Data Not Collected".

Children

The app collects no data, including children's data.

Deleting your data

To delete a game: swipe it to the left in the list, or open it then "···" → "Delete"; the deletion is carried over to your synced devices. Uninstalling the app deletes its data from the device; the copy kept in your iCloud is managed in Settings (your name → iCloud), the publisher holding none.

Changes

If this policy changes, the update date at the top of this page will be changed.
