Put the chalk away: your darts keep score themselves.

Oche Up! keeps score for 501, Cricket, Killer, Shanghai and Around the Clock. Enter your darts by touch or by voice: the app does the maths, suggests checkouts and keeps your stats.

🎯

A pad made for the board

Single, double, triple, bull, undo for the last dart. Two layouts to choose from: classic or X2 / X3. What's left, possible checkouts and busts are handled for you.

🎙️

Voice entry

Say "treble twenty, nineteen, double twelve": the darts appear one by one. Recognition is tuned to the vocabulary of darts, in French and English.

🎲

Six games, many variants

301 to 1001 (double in / out), Cricket (Score, No score, Cut throat), Random Cricket, Around the Clock, Shanghai short or long, Killer single, double or treble, with 3 or 5 lives.

⏱️

Clock and history

Game time and each player's playing time, pause, visit history, corrections and undo. The screen stays on during a game.

📊

Statistics

Averages, best visits, percentages, score evolution, heat map per player. Filter by period, group of players or tournament.

🏆

Tournaments

Group your games into a tournament (a night out, a pub league…), follow the ranking and find the champion.

📄

PDF results

Share the report of a game, a tournament or your statistics: ranking, history, charts.

☁️

iPhone, iPad and iCloud

Your games follow you across your devices through your iCloud. The app is available in French and English.

The games

  • 301, 501, 701, 1001Optional double in and double out · checkout suggestions
  • CricketScore, No score or Cut throat · option to hide useless sectors
  • Random CricketSix random sectors, plus the bull · same variants as Cricket
  • Around the Clock1 to 20 then the bull · standard, or doubles and triples move you forward
  • ShanghaiShort (up to 7) or long (up to 20)
  • KillerSingle, double or treble · 3 or 5 lives · a screen to assign each player a sector

Your data stays yours

Oche Up! has no account and no server. Your games are saved on your device and, if you wish, synced through your own iCloud, which the publisher cannot access. There are no ads and no tracking.
